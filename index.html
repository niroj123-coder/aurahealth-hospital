Welcome to Darwin City Hospital

Established in 1978, Darwin City Hospital serves as the premier public-private healthcare hub for Darwin, Palmerston, and broader Top End communities. Our state-of-the-art facility integrates 14 specialist clinical departments, a 24/7 Level-1 accredited trauma centre, and advanced diagnostic laboratories to deliver compassionate, culturally respectful, and evidence-based patient outcomes.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver safe, high-quality, patient-centred healthcare and emergency services to every resident and visitor across the Northern Territory, irrespective of background.

🌟 Our Vision

To be Australia's leading regional academic hospital, pioneering medical research, digital telemedicine, and advanced clinical training in tropical healthcare.

🛡️ Clinical Governance

Rigorous peer review, multi-disciplinary team audits, and 100% adherence to Australian National Safety and Quality Health Service (NSQHS) standards.

Emergency Medicine & Trauma Centre

Our Emergency Department at Rocklands Drive is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. No referral is necessary for life-threatening medical emergencies.

⚠️ When to Seek Immediate Emergency Care (Call 000):

  • • Crushing chest pain or pressure
  • • Signs of stroke (Face, Arm, Speech, Time)
  • • Severe shortness of breath or choking
  • • Major traumatic injuries or severe bleeding
  • • Sudden loss of consciousness or seizures
  • • Severe envenomation (snake/jellyfish bites)

Gmail SMTP Gateway Configuration

🔑 How to Configure Real Gmail Dispatch (Google App Password)

  1. Open your Google Account (myaccount.google.com) and navigate to the Security tab.
  2. Ensure 2-Step Verification is switched ON.
  3. In the search box at the top, type "App passwords" (or navigate to Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords).
  4. Enter an app name like Darwin City Hospital and click Create.
  5. Google will display a 16-character password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Copy this password.
  6. Open the .env file in this project root and paste your credentials: 
    SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_USER=your-actual-email@gmail.com
SMTP_PASS=abcdefghijklmnop
ADMIN_EMAIL=your-admin-email@gmail.com
  7. Save the .env file and restart the server. Emails will immediately dispatch live to real inboxes!
